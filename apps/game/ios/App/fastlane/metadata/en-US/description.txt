Arrows is a calm, clever puzzle. The board is full of arrow-shaped paths. Tap an arrow whose way out is clear and it slides off the board. Tap one that is blocked and you lose a chance. Clear the board to win.

• Endless levels, from easy to super hard, each a little bigger than the last
• A new daily challenge every day, the same for everyone: a star for every day won, a trophy for every month completed
• A daily league against the game's characters: finish in the top ten to move up, from Bronze to Legend
• Seasonal events with boards drawn as pictures, and a badge for finishing them
• Three chances per board, a timer, and a hint when you are stuck
• Pinch to zoom on the biggest boards
• No account: your progress stays on your phone, and the game plays offline
• In English and Portuguese, with sound, haptics and large text

Every board is made on your phone from a seed, so there is always another one, and every one can be solved.

The game is free and shows ads: one after each board won, and one before each hint.
